About Our School

Established in 2051 B.S. (1994 A.D.), Golden Nature Boarding School is located in the serene surroundings of Khudi-Dhungepatan, Pokhara-30, Kaski. Over decades of dedication, we have grown into a reputable institution providing education from Nursery to Grade 12.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality education that nurtures intellectual, emotional, and social growth, creating future-ready responsible global citizens.

Our Vision

To shape students into knowledgeable, confident, and ethical leaders through academic rigor, modern methods, and moral values.

Our Environment

Situated away from urban pollution, our campus delivers a peaceful, distraction-free environment ideal for deep learning and holistic development.
